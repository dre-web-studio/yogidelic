MEET DRE

yoga for real life

for seven years, i’ve taught yoga and mindfulness in Nashville. often to friends, neighbors, and people in my community who were brand new to the practice, or whose bodies and disabilities made clear how often yoga spaces leave people out.

i’ve spent years learning how to adapt the practice to the body in front of me, rather than asking a body to contort itself to fit an outside expectation. the practice is designed with choice and different bodies in mind. at its heart, Yogidelic is about befriending your inner world and making room to meet yourself as you already are.
